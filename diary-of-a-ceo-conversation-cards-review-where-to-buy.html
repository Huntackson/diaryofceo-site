What Are the Diary of a CEO Conversation Cards?

The cards are a deck of thought-provoking questions designed to spark deeper, more meaningful conversations. They come in a sleek matte-black box that looks premium on a coffee table. There are multiple editions � the original, a couples edition, and a family-friendly version.

Each card contains a single question. No rules, no scoring, no winners. You just pick a card, read the question out loud, and everyone answers. That's it. And somehow, that simplicity is exactly what makes them work.

What Questions Are on the Cards?

Without spoiling specific cards, the questions fall into several categories:

Tips for Getting the Most Out of Them

After dozens of sessions, here's what I've learned works best:

  • Set the mood first. Low lighting, no phones, maybe wine. The environment matters as much as the questions.
  • The person who reads the card answers first. This creates vulnerability that gives others permission to be honest.
  • Skip cards freely. Not every question fits every group. No guilt about passing.
  • Limit to 5-7 cards per session. Going too long dilutes the quality. Leave people wanting more.
  • Use them solo. Journal your answers. Some of the best insights come from sitting alone with these questions.
