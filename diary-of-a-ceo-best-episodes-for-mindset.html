Diary of a CEO Best Episodes for Mindset — 12 Episodes That Will Rewire Your Brain (2026)

Steven Bartlett's Diary of a CEO has hosted some of the world's greatest thinkers on psychology, habits, and mental performance. If you want to upgrade your mindset — how you think about failure, success, discipline, and growth — these are the 12 episodes you need to listen to. Each one is packed with actionable frameworks you can apply immediately.

⏱️ Reading time: 8 minutes  |  📅 Updated: February 2026  |  🎧 Over 1.5 hours of curated wisdom per episode

Why These 12 Episodes?

We ranked these episodes based on three criteria: depth of mindset insights, practical actionability, and listener impact (measured by views, shares, and community feedback). Every episode on this list has at least one framework you can implement today.

1

James Clear — Atomic Habits & The Power of Identity-Based Change

Guest: James Clear, Author of Atomic Habits

James Clear's conversation with Steven is a masterclass in behavior change. Rather than focusing on goals, Clear argues that your habits shape your identity — and your identity shapes your habits. The key insight: don't aim to "run a marathon." Aim to become "a runner." This subtle shift changes everything.

"Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become." — James Clear, Author of Atomic Habits

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • 1% better daily — tiny improvements compound into remarkable results over months
  • Identity-first habits — focus on who you want to become, not what you want to achieve
  • Environment design — make good habits obvious and bad habits invisible
  • The 2-minute rule — scale any habit down to just 2 minutes to eliminate resistance
2

Jordan Peterson — 12 Rules for Life & Psychological Resilience

Guest: Jordan Peterson, Clinical Psychologist & Professor

Peterson's episode is intense, emotional, and deeply philosophical. He and Steven discuss why voluntary suffering builds character, why you should "compare yourself to who you were yesterday, not who someone else is today," and how to find meaning even in the darkest moments of life.

"You're not everything you could be, and you know it. And that gap between what you are and what you could be — that's where meaning lives." — Jordan Peterson, Clinical Psychologist

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Embrace responsibility — meaning comes from carrying a heavy load voluntarily
  • Order vs. chaos — the sweet spot for growth is one foot in order, one foot in chaos
  • Tell the truth — or at least don't lie; self-deception is the enemy of growth
  • Clean your room — start with what's in front of you before trying to change the world
3

Dr. Carol Dweck — The Woman Who Discovered Growth Mindset

Guest: Dr. Carol Dweck, Stanford Psychologist

This is the definitive conversation on growth mindset from the researcher who coined the term. Dr. Dweck explains why praising effort over talent creates resilient children (and adults), and reveals common misconceptions about growth mindset — including the "false growth mindset" trap many people fall into.

"The passion for stretching yourself and sticking to it, even when it's not going well, is the hallmark of the growth mindset." — Dr. Carol Dweck, Stanford Psychologist

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Fixed vs. growth — believing abilities are developable (not fixed) unlocks potential
  • Praise the process — celebrate effort, strategy, and progress, not innate talent
  • "Not yet" power — adding "yet" to "I can't do this" transforms your relationship with failure
  • Trigger awareness — notice when you slip into fixed-mindset thinking under pressure
4

Mel Robbins — The 5-Second Rule & Overcoming Self-Doubt

Guest: Mel Robbins, Motivational Speaker & Author

Mel Robbins gets raw about her struggles with anxiety, self-doubt, and feeling like a failure. Her solution is disarmingly simple: count 5-4-3-2-1 and move. It's a "starting ritual" that interrupts hesitation and activates the prefrontal cortex. Steven called this one of the most practically useful conversations he's ever had.

"You are one decision away from a completely different life. But you have to make that decision within five seconds or your brain will kill it." — Mel Robbins, Author & Speaker

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • The 5-second window — act within 5 seconds of an impulse before your brain talks you out of it
  • Motivation is garbage — stop waiting to feel ready; action creates motivation, not vice versa
  • High-five habit — build self-belief through small daily rituals of self-encouragement
  • Reframe anxiety as excitement — they have the same physiological signature
5

Chris Williamson — Modern Masculinity & Building Mental Models

Guest: Chris Williamson, Host of Modern Wisdom Podcast

This episode is a deep dive into how to think about thinking. Chris Williamson and Steven discuss cognitive biases, building mental models, the loneliness epidemic, and why most people's lives don't change because they never question their default assumptions about reality.

"Most people don't have an information problem. They have an action problem. They know what to do — they just don't do it." — Chris Williamson, Host of Modern Wisdom

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Question defaults — most of your beliefs were installed, not chosen
  • First-principles thinking — break problems down to fundamentals instead of reasoning by analogy
  • Social environment — you become the average of the content you consume and people you spend time with
6

Dr. Andrew Huberman — The Neuroscience of Mindset, Focus & Drive

Guest: Dr. Andrew Huberman, Stanford Neuroscientist

Huberman brings hard science to mindset. He explains how dopamine drives motivation (and how most people unknowingly destroy their dopamine system), how to enter flow states on command, and why your morning sunlight exposure literally programs your brain's performance for the day.

"Dopamine is not about pleasure. It's about the pursuit. The moment you get the reward, dopamine drops. The craving is the point." — Dr. Andrew Huberman, Stanford Neuroscientist

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Protect your dopamine — avoid cheap dopamine hits (social media, sugar) to maintain drive
  • Morning sunlight — 10 minutes of early light sets your circadian rhythm and cortisol spike
  • Deliberate cold exposure — boosts baseline dopamine by 250% for hours
  • Focus protocols — 90-minute deep work blocks aligned with ultradian rhythms
7

Alex Hormozi — The $100M Mindset: Volume, Patience & Skill Stacking

Guest: Alex Hormozi, Entrepreneur & Author

Hormozi's episode cuts through motivational fluff with brutal clarity. His core message: most people fail because they quit too early and do too little. He shares how he built a $100M+ portfolio by doing 10x the volume everyone else does, and why "being willing to be bad at something for long enough" is the real secret to mastery.

"The person who can endure the most boredom wins. Entrepreneurship isn't about excitement — it's about doing boring things consistently for an unreasonable amount of time." — Alex Hormozi, Entrepreneur

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Volume is king — do more than everyone else; quantity creates quality
  • Seasons of life — sacrifice now so you have options later; delayed gratification at scale
  • Skill stacking — become top 10% in 3 skills rather than top 1% in one
8

Matthew McConaughey — Greenlights, Finding Your Path & Earned Wisdom

Guest: Matthew McConaughey, Actor & Author

McConaughey shares lessons from his memoir Greenlights — how red lights and yellow lights eventually turn green if you keep moving. He discusses his decision to step away from Hollywood at the peak of rom-com fame to reinvent himself, proving that sometimes you have to lose to win.

"Life's not fair. It never was, it isn't now, and it won't ever be. But do not fall into the trap of thinking that because it isn't fair, it isn't good." — Matthew McConaughey, Actor & Author

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Greenlights philosophy — obstacles are future greenlights in disguise
  • Unbelievable is the stupidest word — expand your sense of what's possible
  • Journal everything — McConaughey kept 36 years of journals that became his memoir

9

Jay Shetty — From Monk to Media Mogul: Purpose, Presence & Peace

Guest: Jay Shetty, Author & Former Monk

Jay Shetty spent three years as a monk in India before becoming one of the most-followed creators online. His conversation with Steven explores how monk principles apply to modern life — detachment from outcomes, finding your dharma (purpose), and why stillness is a superpower in a noisy world.

"You're not what happened to you. You're what you choose to become from what happened to you." — Jay Shetty, Author & Former Monk

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Detach from outcomes — focus on the effort, let go of the result
  • Morning intention — start each day with a clear purpose, not your phone
  • Service mindset — the fastest path to fulfillment is helping others
10

Dr. Julie Smith — Rewiring Negative Thought Patterns

Guest: Dr. Julie Smith, Clinical Psychologist

Dr. Julie Smith became TikTok's most-followed psychologist for a reason — she makes complex psychology accessible. In this episode, she explains cognitive distortions (the lies your brain tells you), how to interrupt anxiety spirals, and practical CBT techniques anyone can use without a therapist.

"Your thoughts are not facts. Learning to observe them instead of believe them is the single most important mental health skill." — Dr. Julie Smith, Clinical Psychologist

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Thought defusion — observe thoughts like clouds passing, don't engage with every one
  • Behavioral activation — when depressed, action precedes motivation (not the other way around)
  • Name it to tame it — labeling an emotion reduces its power over you
11

Simon Sinek — Start With Why, Infinite Games & The Courage to Lead

Guest: Simon Sinek, Author & Leadership Expert

Sinek reframes success entirely. Instead of playing to "win," he argues for an infinite mindset — playing to keep playing. He challenges Steven on what it really means to lead, why vulnerability is strength, and how most businesses fail because they optimize for finite goals in an infinite game.

"Working hard for something we don't care about is called stress. Working hard for something we love is called passion." — Simon Sinek, Author of Start With Why

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Infinite mindset — play long-term games; outlast, don't outwin
  • Start with why — clarity of purpose drives everything downstream
  • Vulnerability as strength — admitting "I don't know" builds more trust than pretending
12

Steven Bartlett — His Own Mindset Journey: From Dropout to £400M CEO

Host Spotlight: Steven Bartlett, Founder of Social Chain

Some of the best mindset content on DOAC comes from Steven himself — particularly in solo episodes and opening monologues. His journey from dropping out of university with nothing to building Social Chain and becoming the youngest-ever Dragon on Dragons' Den is itself a mindset case study in betting on yourself when nobody else will.

"I didn't have a plan B. And I think that's important, because plan B is often a distraction from plan A." — Steven Bartlett, Founder of Social Chain

🧠 Key Mindset Takeaways

  • Burn the boats — full commitment beats hedged bets
  • Reframe rejection — every "no" is data, not a verdict
  • Fill your five buckets — knowledge, skills, network, resources, reputation

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Diary of a CEO episode for beginners?

Start with James Clear's episode on Atomic Habits (#1 on our list). It's accessible, practical, and gives you a framework you can apply to any area of your life immediately. Mel Robbins (#4) is also a great starting point if you struggle with procrastination.

How long are Diary of a CEO episodes?

Most episodes are about 1.5 hours long, though some deep conversations run up to 2 hours. Steven allows guests to go deep rather than cutting conversations short — which is what makes the mindset episodes so valuable.

Are Diary of a CEO mindset episodes evidence-based?

Many are. Episodes with Dr. Carol Dweck, Dr. Andrew Huberman, and Dr. Julie Smith feature researchers discussing peer-reviewed science. Other episodes (Hormozi, McConaughey) are more experiential but equally insightful for practical application.

What's the difference between mindset and motivation episodes?

Mindset episodes focus on how you think — your mental frameworks, beliefs, and cognitive patterns. Motivation episodes focus on getting fired up. We prioritize mindset because it creates lasting change, while motivation fades. That said, many episodes on this list (like Mel Robbins) deliver both.

Does Diary of a CEO have a mindset playlist?

There's no official playlist, but this list serves as the definitive guide. We've curated these 12 episodes specifically for mindset transformation. Bookmark this page and work through them in order for the best results.
