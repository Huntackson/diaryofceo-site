How to Use This Guest List

With hundreds of episodes, the Diary of a CEO can feel overwhelming. Here's how to navigate it:

  • Pick your current challenge. Struggling with money? Start with Hormozi and Housel. Going through a breakup? Esther Perel and Matthew Hussey. Feeling lost? Jay Shetty and Jordan Peterson.
  • Listen to one per week. These are 1.5-hour deep conversations, not 10-minute snippets. Give each episode the attention it deserves.
  • Take notes. The guests who appear on DOAC share frameworks, not platitudes. Write down the one thing you'll implement from each episode.

The guest list continues to grow every week. New episodes drop on YouTube and all major podcast platforms, typically on Mondays and Thursdays.
