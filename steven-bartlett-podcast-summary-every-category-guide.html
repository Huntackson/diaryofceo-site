What Is The Diary of a CEO?

The Diary of a CEO (DOAC) is a long-form interview podcast hosted by Steven Bartlett, a British-Nigerian entrepreneur who became the youngest ever investor on BBC's Dragons' Den. The show launched in 2017 as a business-focused interview series but has since expanded into health, relationships, psychology, science, and personal development.

What sets the podcast apart from competitors like Joe Rogan or Lex Fridman is Bartlett's unique combination: he's young enough (33) to be relatable to a millennial/Gen Z audience, wealthy enough ($400M+ net worth) to attract A-list guests, and emotionally intelligent enough to ask the questions nobody else does.

"I don't want to be the smartest person in the room. I want to be the most curious. Because the most curious person always leaves the room smarter than everyone else."

— Steven Bartlett, Host of The Diary of a CEO

The show films in a distinctive dark studio with Bartlett's signature opening: asking each guest to fill in a statement from the "previous guest's question." This passing-the-baton format creates a sense of continuity across episodes.

🏢 Business & Entrepreneurship Episodes

Overview

The original backbone of the podcast. These episodes feature founders, CEOs, and investors sharing the unfiltered truth about building companies. Unlike most business podcasts, DOAC focuses on the emotional and psychological side of entrepreneurship — the loneliness, the imposter syndrome, the personal cost.

Top 5 Must-Listen Business Episodes

  1. Alex Hormozi — "$100M Offers" — The Gym Launch founder breaks down how to create offers so good people feel stupid saying no. His value equation (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood / Time × Effort) has become a business meme.
  2. Sara Blakely — "Building Spanx With $5,000" — The billionaire founder's story of cold-calling hosiery mills and being laughed out of every room. Her lesson: "My dad used to ask 'what did you fail at this week?' Failure was celebrated."
  3. Gary Vaynerchuk — "The Harsh Truth About Business" — Gary Vee brings his trademark intensity to discuss patience as the ultimate competitive advantage. "Everyone wants to be an entrepreneur until they realize it means 10 years of nobody caring."
  4. Daniel Priestley — "The Entrepreneur's Roadmap" — A structured, frameworks-heavy episode perfect for early-stage founders. His "Key Person of Influence" method is immediately actionable.
  5. Shaan Puri — "The New Rules of Making Money Online" — A masterclass in modern business building, from newsletters to communities to AI tools.

"Most people overestimate what they can do in one year and underestimate what they can do in ten years."

— Alex Hormozi, Entrepreneur, on The Diary of a CEO

Key Themes: Value creation, brand building, resilience through failure, scaling systems, personal brand as leverage.

🏥 Health, Fitness & Nutrition Episodes

Overview

Health episodes have become DOAC's biggest growth engine. Episodes with Dr. Andrew Huberman, Dr. Chris van Tulleken, and Professor Matthew Walker have each surpassed 20 million views. Bartlett's health content stands out because he asks practical "what should I actually do?" questions rather than getting lost in scientific jargon.

Top 5 Must-Listen Health Episodes

  1. Dr. Chris van Tulleken — "Ultra-Processed Food Is Destroying Your Body" — The most viral health episode in DOAC history. Van Tulleken's experiment of eating 80% ultra-processed food for a month — and the brain scans showing addiction-like changes — shocked millions.
  2. Andrew Huberman — "Neuroscience of Optimal Performance" — Protocols for sleep, focus, and stress management backed by Stanford research. Practical and specific.
  3. Matthew Walker — "The Sleep Episode" — Every claim backed by data. Walker makes the case that sleep deprivation is the silent killer of modern society.
  4. Dr. Tim Spector — "The Food You Should Be Eating" — The gut microbiome pioneer explains why personalised nutrition matters more than generic diets. His 30-plant-per-week challenge has become a health meme.
  5. Dr. Rangan Chatterjee — "The 4 Pillars of Health" — A practical framework: food, movement, sleep, relaxation. Simple and applicable.

💕 Relationships & Dating Episodes

Overview

These episodes consistently generate the most comments and debate. Bartlett is refreshingly honest about his own relationship failures, which creates space for guests to go deeper than surface-level dating advice.

Top 5 Must-Listen Relationship Episodes

  1. Esther Perel — "The Secret to Desire in Long-Term Relationships" — The world's most famous relationship therapist explains the paradox: we want security AND mystery in the same relationship. Her insight that "the quality of our relationships determines the quality of our lives" is the episode's thesis.
  2. Matthew Hussey — "How to Get the Love You Want" — Practical dating strategies that aren't manipulative. Hussey's framework for confident communication is gold.
  3. Dr. John Gottman — "The Science of Love" — Four decades of marriage research distilled into one conversation. Gottman can predict divorce with 94% accuracy by watching couples interact for 15 minutes.
  4. Derren Brown — "The Psychology of Attraction" — Mind-bending insights on how our subconscious chooses partners. Not what you expect from a mentalist.
  5. Steven Bartlett Solo — "What I Learned From My Breakup" — Raw, vulnerable, and surprisingly tactical. Steven deconstructs attachment styles and how his avoidant patterns destroyed his relationship.

🧠 Mindset & Psychology Episodes

Overview

The crossover category. Mindset episodes blend psychology, neuroscience, and personal development. These are the episodes listeners report having the most lasting impact on their daily lives.

Top 5 Must-Listen Mindset Episodes

  1. Dr. Gabor Maté — "The Root Cause of Every Problem" — Trauma, addiction, and self-worth explored with breathtaking depth. This episode has made more people book therapy appointments than any self-help book.
  2. Jay Shetty — "Think Like a Monk in a Modern World" — Practical meditation and mindfulness frameworks from a former monk turned content creator.
  3. Mo Gawdat — "The Happiness Equation" — The former Google X chief business officer created a mathematical formula for happiness after the death of his son. Devastating and hopeful.
  4. Jordan Peterson — "12 Rules for Life" — Love him or hate him, Peterson's clinical insights on responsibility, meaning, and suffering are intellectually rigorous.
  5. David Goggins — "The 40% Rule" — Pure mental fortitude. Goggins' philosophy that suffering is the pathway to mental freedom challenges every comfort-seeking instinct.

💰 Money & Wealth Episodes

Overview

Money episodes on DOAC go beyond "save more, spend less." Bartlett brings on self-made billionaires, investment legends, and financial psychologists who explain the mindset shifts required to build real wealth.

Top 5 Must-Listen Money Episodes

  1. Morgan Housel — "The Psychology of Money" — The best-selling author explains why financial success is more about behaviour than intelligence. His concept of "wealth is what you don't see" reframes the entire money conversation.
  2. Ramit Sethi — "I Will Teach You to Be Rich" — Actionable, no-shame money advice. Sethi challenges the "skip the latte" mentality and focuses on the Big Wins: salary negotiation, automation, and investing.
  3. Naval Ravikant — "How to Get Rich Without Getting Lucky" — Naval's frameworks on specific knowledge, leverage, and accountability are the most intellectually powerful wealth-building concepts on the show.
  4. Codie Sanchez — "Boring Businesses That Print Money" — Laundromats, car washes, and vending machines. Sanchez makes a compelling case for buying boring businesses over starting flashy startups.
  5. Steven Bartlett Solo — "Everything I Know About Money" — Steven shares his actual portfolio allocation, his mistakes, and his rules for wealth building. Rare transparency from someone worth $400M+.

"Wealth is not about having a lot of money. It's about having a lot of options."

— Morgan Housel, Author of The Psychology of Money, on The Diary of a CEO

🔬 Science & Human Performance Episodes

Overview

A newer category that's growing fast. These episodes feature cutting-edge researchers explaining longevity, genetics, brain science, and human potential. They appeal to the "optimiser" audience that wants evidence-based protocols.

Top 3 Must-Listen Science Episodes

  1. Dr. Peter Attia — "The Science of Longevity" — Attia breaks down the four horsemen of chronic disease (heart disease, cancer, neurodegenerative disease, metabolic dysfunction) and what to do about each. His framework for "healthspan" vs "lifespan" is essential.
  2. Bryan Johnson — "The Man Trying to Live Forever" — The tech millionaire spending $2M/year on anti-aging protocols. Controversial, fascinating, and surprisingly practical (his sleep and supplement protocols are accessible).
  3. Prof. Hannah Fry — "The Mathematics of Love and Life" — How math can predict relationship success, optimise decision-making, and explain human behaviour. Unexpected and delightful.

How The Diary of a CEO Has Evolved

The podcast has gone through three distinct eras:

Era 1 (2017-2019): The Business Show. Early episodes focused almost exclusively on entrepreneurship. Guests were mostly UK business figures. Production quality was modest. These episodes are still valuable but feel smaller in scope.

Era 2 (2020-2022): The Breakout. COVID lockdowns drove podcast consumption to all-time highs, and DOAC capitalised with bigger guests and broader topics. The addition of health and relationship content tripled the audience.

Era 3 (2023-Present): The Global Platform. DOAC is now a top-10 global podcast. Guests include Nobel Prize winners, Hollywood actors, world leaders, and scientists. Production is cinematic. Bartlett's interviewing has matured dramatically — he listens more, pushes harder, and lets silences sit.

Who Is Steven Bartlett?

Steven Bartlett was born in Botswana to a Nigerian mother and British father. He grew up in Plymouth, England, dropped out of Manchester Metropolitan University at 18, and started Social Chain from his bedroom in 2014. The company went public in 2019, making Bartlett a millionaire in his mid-twenties.

He became the youngest Dragon on BBC's Dragons' Den in 2022 at age 29. His investment portfolio includes Huel, Zoe, and dozens of tech startups through his venture firm Flight Story. His estimated net worth exceeds $400 million.

But what makes Bartlett compelling as a podcast host isn't his wealth — it's his vulnerability. He openly discusses his ADHD diagnosis, his struggles with loneliness, his fear of abandonment rooted in his childhood, and his ongoing therapy journey. This emotional honesty is what separates DOAC from more polished competitors.

Where to Start: Your Personalised Listening Plan

Don't try to listen to everything. Pick your priority and start there:

  • 📈 Want to make money? Start with Morgan Housel → Alex Hormozi → Naval Ravikant
  • 🏋️ Want to get healthy? Start with Matthew Walker → Chris van Tulleken → Andrew Huberman
  • ❤️ Want better relationships? Start with Esther Perel → Gabor Maté → Matthew Hussey
  • 🧠 Want a stronger mindset? Start with James Clear → David Goggins → Jordan Peterson
  • 🔬 Want to optimise performance? Start with Peter Attia → Andrew Huberman → Bryan Johnson

Each path gives you roughly 6-8 hours of focused content. That's the equivalent of reading 3-4 books — but free, and arguably more engaging because of Bartlett's interviewing.
