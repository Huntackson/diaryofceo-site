How to Choose Your First Episode

With 400+ episodes, starting can feel overwhelming. Here's a simple framework:

  1. Start with your biggest struggle. Stressed about money? Start with the finance guests. Struggling in a relationship? Jump to Esther Perel or Matthew Hussey. Can't sleep? Dr. Matthew Walker will change your life.
  2. Try a celebrity episode first. If you're new to the podcast, celebrity episodes are often the most accessible entry point. They tend to be more personal and story-driven.
  3. Browse by category above. Find a name you recognise or a topic you care about.
  4. Check our curated lists. We've ranked the best episodes across every major category on diaryofceo.online.
