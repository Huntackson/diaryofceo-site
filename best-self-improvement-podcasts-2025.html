How We Ranked the Best Self Improvement Podcasts 2025

Not all self improvement podcasts are created equal. We evaluated each show on five criteria:

  • Actionability � Does it give you things to do, not just think about?
  • Guest quality � Are the guests actual experts or just celebrities promoting books?
  • Consistency � Is the show still actively publishing quality episodes in 2025?
  • Production value � Audio quality, editing, structure matter for retention.
  • Depth � Does it go beyond surface-level advice?

The 15 Best Self Improvement Podcasts of 2025

1. The Diary of a CEO with Steven Bartlett

No list of the best self improvement podcasts 2025 would be complete without Diary of a CEO. Steven Bartlett's interview style extracts insights from guests that you simply won't hear anywhere else. From neuroscientists like Andrew Huberman to business titans like Alex Hormozi, every episode delivers frameworks you can apply the same day.

Why it's #1: The combination of world-class guests, deep research, and Bartlett's willingness to ask uncomfortable questions puts this show in a category of its own. Episodes regularly cross 2+ hours but never feel padded.

Start with: The James Clear Atomic Habits episode or the Andrew Huberman neuroscience deep dive.

2. Huberman Lab

Andrew Huberman turned neuroscience into something accessible without dumbing it down. His solo episodes on dopamine, sleep, focus, and exercise are essentially free university lectures backed by peer-reviewed research. In 2025, the show expanded to include more guest interviews while maintaining its scientific rigor.

Best for: Anyone who wants evidence-based protocols for sleep, focus, and physical health.

3. The School of Greatness with Lewis Howes

Lewis Howes has been in the self improvement podcast space for over a decade, and his show keeps evolving. The 2025 season leans heavily into emotional intelligence, vulnerability, and redefining masculinity � topics many male-oriented podcasts avoid.

Best for: Men looking for a well-rounded perspective on success and emotional growth.

4. On Purpose with Jay Shetty

Jay Shetty bridges Eastern philosophy and Western productivity in a way that feels genuine, not performative. His Monday motivation episodes are short enough for a commute, while Thursday interviews go deep with guests like mindfulness experts and thought leaders.

Best for: Listeners who want mindfulness woven into practical life advice.

5. The Mel Robbins Podcast

Mel Robbins exploded in 2024-2025 with her "Let Them Theory" concept, and her podcast reflects that momentum. Episodes are tight, well-structured, and always end with a clear action step. Her Diary of a CEO appearance is also a must-listen.

Best for: People who need a kick to start taking action � not more theory.

6. Modern Wisdom with Chris Williamson

Chris Williamson tackles self improvement from every angle � psychology, fitness, dating, philosophy, and culture. His crossover episodes with Diary of a CEO are some of the most-listened-to podcast content of the year.

Best for: Intellectually curious people who want to explore ideas beyond typical self-help.

7. The Tim Ferriss Show

The godfather of self improvement podcasts is still going strong. Ferriss's meticulous interview prep means guests share things they've never shared before. His "Tools of Titans" style questions remain the gold standard for extracting actionable routines.

Best for: High-achievers who want to reverse-engineer success habits. See also: Tim Ferriss on Diary of a CEO.

8. Impact Theory with Tom Bilyeu

Tom Bilyeu brings an entrepreneur's lens to personal development. His episodes on mindset, discipline, and building mental toughness resonate strongly with anyone trying to level up professionally while maintaining their mental health.

Best for: Entrepreneurs who see self improvement as a business advantage.

9. Feel Better, Live More with Dr. Rangan Chatterjee

Dr. Chatterjee approaches self improvement through the lens of holistic health � sleep, nutrition, movement, and stress management. His gentle interview style makes even heavy topics accessible. The Diary of a CEO crossover episode is essential listening.

Best for: Anyone who wants to improve their life starting with their body and health.

10. The Knowledge Project with Shane Parrish

Shane Parrish, founder of Farnam Street, hosts the most intellectually rigorous self improvement podcast available. Episodes focus on decision-making, mental models, and clear thinking � skills that compound across every area of life.

Best for: Analytical thinkers who want to improve how they think, not just what they do.

11. The Mindset Mentor with Rob Dial

Short, punchy episodes (15-25 minutes) make this one of the best self improvement podcasts 2025 for people short on time. Rob Dial covers one topic per episode with clear takeaways and zero filler.

Best for: Commuters and busy professionals who want daily mindset shifts in bite-sized format.

12. Jocko Podcast

Jocko Willink's podcast isn't for everyone � it's intense, military-influenced, and unapologetically about discipline. But if you need someone to cut through your excuses, there's no better voice in podcasting. His philosophy of "discipline equals freedom" has influenced millions, including multiple Diary of a CEO guests.

Best for: People who respond to tough love and accountability.

13. Happier with Gretchen Rubin

Gretchen Rubin's research-backed approach to habits and happiness is refreshingly practical. Her "Four Tendencies" framework helps you understand why certain self improvement strategies work for some people but not others.

Best for: Habit-builders who want a personality-driven approach to change.

14. The Rich Roll Podcast

Rich Roll's transformation from overweight lawyer to ultra-endurance athlete gives him a unique perspective on change. His long-form interviews (often 2-3 hours) cover plant-based nutrition, endurance mindset, creativity, and spiritual growth.

Best for: Listeners who enjoy deep, contemplative conversations about transformation.

15. We Can Do Hard Things with Glennon Doyle

Glennon Doyle brings raw vulnerability to topics like relationships, identity, parenting, and resilience. The podcast gained massive traction in 2025 for its honest approach to the messy side of personal growth.

Best for: Women (and men) who want self improvement that embraces imperfection.

How to Actually Learn From Self Improvement Podcasts

Listening to the best self improvement podcasts 2025 offers means nothing if you don't apply what you hear. Here's a simple system:

  • One podcast, one action. After each episode, write down ONE thing you'll implement.
  • Use summaries. Sites like diaryofceo.online provide episode summaries so you can revisit key lessons without re-listening.
  • Batch by topic. Don't listen randomly � pick a theme (discipline, health, money) and listen to 3-5 episodes on that topic across different shows.
  • Share what you learn. Teaching someone else what you learned cements it in your memory.
