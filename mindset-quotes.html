Top Diary of a CEO Quotes That Will Change Your Mindset

Updated March 2026 • 9 min read • By DiaryOfCEO.online

The Diary of a CEO isn't just a podcast — it's a masterclass in human psychology, delivered one conversation at a time. Over hundreds of episodes, Steven Bartlett and his guests have dropped quotes so powerful they stop you mid-step on your morning walk.

We've curated the best Diary of a CEO quotes that genuinely shift how you think about success, failure, money, love, and life. These aren't motivational fluff. They're hard-earned truths from people who've lived them.

Quotes on Success and Ambition

"You don't need everyone to love you. You need a few people to love you a lot. That's how you build a business, a brand, and a life."

— Steven Bartlett, Diary of a CEO

"The thing that separates successful people from everyone else isn't talent or luck. It's that they kept going when it stopped being fun."

— Steven Bartlett, Diary of a CEO

"Your competition isn't other people. Your competition is your distractions, your ego, and your lack of commitment."

— Alex Hormozi, Guest on DOAC

"Most people overestimate what they can do in a day and underestimate what they can do in a year."

— Gary Vaynerchuk, Guest on DOAC

These quotes capture a truth that appears across nearly every billionaire interview on the show. For a deeper dive into these patterns, read our piece on what Steven Bartlett learned from billionaires.

Quotes on Failure and Resilience

"Failure is just data. The people who succeed aren't the ones who avoid failure — they're the ones who extract the lesson fastest."

— Steven Bartlett, Diary of a CEO

"Rock bottom became the solid foundation on which I rebuilt my life."

— J.K. Rowling, as referenced on DOAC

"The moment you accept full responsibility for your life is the moment you gain the power to change it."

— Steven Bartlett, Diary of a CEO

"Every successful person I've met has been through hell. The difference is they didn't stay there."

— Steven Bartlett, Diary of a CEO

Failure is a recurring theme across the show's most popular episodes. Steven himself dropped out of university and built his first company from nothing — a story he's told with remarkable honesty across many episodes.

"The best investment you can make is in yourself. Knowledge compounds faster than any stock."

"Most people will spend more time planning their holiday than planning their financial future. That's why most people are broke."

— Codie Sanchez, Guest on DOAC

— Brené Brown, Guest on DOAC

How to Use These Quotes

Reading quotes is easy. Letting them change your behavior is the hard part. Here's how to make these Diary of a CEO quotes actually stick:

  • Pick one quote per week. Write it on a sticky note. Put it where you'll see it every morning.
  • Journal on it. Spend five minutes writing about how that quote applies to your current situation.
  • Share it. Teaching is the fastest way to internalize an idea. Text a quote to someone who needs to hear it.
  • Listen to the full episode. Quotes are seeds. The full conversation is the soil. Visit our episode summaries for context.

The Diary of a CEO has grown into the world's most-listened-to podcast for a reason — it delivers wisdom that actually changes lives. These quotes are just the starting point. The real transformation happens when you listen, reflect, and act.
