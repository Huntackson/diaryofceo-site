Tweet 2 (40% Rule)

🔥 The 40% Rule:

"When your mind says you're done, you're only 40% done."

Your brain quits before your body does. Every. Single. Time.

The discomfort you feel isn't your limit — it's your brain's first line of defense.

Push past it. That's where growth lives.

Tweet 3 (Accountability Mirror)

Most people use their mirror for affirmations.

Goggins uses his for the truth.

He put sticky notes on his mirror listing every lie he was telling himself — every excuse, every failure, every area where he was falling short.

Then he read them out loud. Every. Morning.

That's how a 297-pound exterminator became a Navy SEAL.

Tweet 4 (Discipline vs Motivation)

"Motivation is garbage."

Goggins doesn't wait to feel inspired. He performs whether he feels like it or not.

His standard: "In combat, nobody asks if you feel motivated."

Stop waiting for the right mood.

Stop optimizing for comfort.

Discipline is doing it when you hate it.
