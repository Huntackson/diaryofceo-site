📅 DOAC 2026 Release Schedule

The biggest structural change to Diary of a CEO in 2026 is its full-scale US operation. Bartlett split his time between London and LA throughout 2025, but 2026 marks the first year with a permanent American production hub.

What this means for listeners:

  • Bigger American guests: Proximity matters in podcast land. Being based in LA means easier access to Hollywood, Silicon Valley, and the broader American media ecosystem.
